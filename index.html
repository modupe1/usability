Load your own slices from the export cell of the notebook

Select the PNG files from explorer_export: each slice needs …_I.png (normalized image), …_S.png (vesselness) and …_M.png (vessel mask). The files stay in your browser.
The built-in real examples are two axial slices of ICBM MRA volume BG0006 (healthy; a held-out volume of the benchmark) and two aneurysm cases from the Lausanne TOF-MRA cohort (OpenNeuro ds003949; slice and thin slab MIP through the aneurysm, with the dataset's aneurysm label as an optional yellow outline), all exported with the paper's own normalization, vesselness and vessel mask; they open with a wider display window (level 0.6, width 1.4) so that the brightest vessels are not clipped. The synthetic phantom has a circle-of-Willis-like vessel tree and one saccular aneurysm (low vesselness, so it takes a different hue). Metrics follow the paper in 2-D: ΔE d′ uses CIE76 distances to the mean ring color; vessel order inversion is the share of 20,000 sampled vessel-voxel pairs whose Rec. 709 luminance order disagrees with the grayscale order; local contrast is mean vessel luminance minus mean ring luminance; visible color is the share of vessel voxels with C* above 2.3 CIELAB units on a calibrated sRGB display. The soft weight is smoothed within the slice, whereas the paper smooths in 3-D.
